## Working proposition
**User:** QA/training or operations manager at an outsourced customer-support team. **Job:** Before publishing a revised refund/escalation policy, compare decisions on previously approved boundary and exception scenarios. **Position:** human-approved policy test bench, not an autonomous compliance auditor.

## Rules
- Real customers, independently measured usefulness and collected revenue are stronger evidence than architecture or GitHub activity.
- Differentiate idea, demo, test receipt, independent validation and customer success.
- Prior failures remain visible. No invented pilot results, AI reliability, legal certifications, revenue or endorsements.
- Do not commit client materials, company SOPs, credentials, private finances, interview identities or employment documents to this public repository.
- GitHub issues are assignments, not completed results; close with evidence linked.
- Re-evaluate the idea at documented stage gates. Stop/pivot is a valid outcome.
