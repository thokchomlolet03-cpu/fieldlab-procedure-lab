# 01 — Company charter
## Vision
Help operational teams test the consequences of written procedures before those procedures cause inconsistent decisions.

## The smallest valid startup thesis
A support-team QA manager who frequently updates policies will repeatedly use and eventually pay for an affordable tool that stores expected decisions and replays realistic edge cases after each policy revision. **Unverified until interviews, pilots and payments.**

## Founder–problem fit
The founder has experience with customer service, enterprise IT support, process rules, escalation, regulated operations and shift supervision, plus independent research on evidence and uncertainty. This supports the right to investigate, NOT a claim to understand all buyers or to have validated a market.

## Target and boundary
- First workflow: refund eligibility and supervisor escalation policies.
- First users: QA/training and operations managers in BPO/customer-support teams.
- First inputs: short text and fictional or permissioned, sanitized cases.
- Reviewer owns interpretation and expected outcome; AI may propose hypotheses but cannot approve rules.
- Avoid legal compliance certification, medical/financial client records, dangerous instructions, surveillance of employees, and fully autonomous decisions.

## Founding principles
1. The customer problem outranks founder attachment to a solution.
2. Talk to users and ship small improvements every week, within sustainable part-time capacity.
3. Write down what evidence would disprove an assumption before building.
4. Preserve negative results, uncertainties, first attempts and source links.
5. Never confuse passing a unit test with a positive business outcome.
6. Do not put proprietary employer/client data into demos or AI services.
7. A smaller product that solves one paid problem is better than a grand universal agent with no customers.
8. Review IP, employment, business authorization and privacy obligations before collecting actual customer data or revenue.

## Constraints
Initial capacity assumption: 6–8 focused hours/week around rotating work shifts; reassess monthly. Keep research, employment, household finances and health sustainable. No assumption that anticipated future household earnings fund the venture. Mangal remains a separate inquiry project; reuse its methods only when they measurably help customers.

## What must be earned
Proof of recurring costly problem; credible buyer and budget; external usage; differentiated outcome versus spreadsheet/general AI; paid pilot; repeat retention. YC acceptance and relocation to New York are possible future consequences, not product requirements.
