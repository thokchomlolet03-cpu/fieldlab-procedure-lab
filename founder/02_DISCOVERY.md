# 02 — Customer discovery and market validation
**Stage:** Zero verified paying customers. Do not code your way around missing demand.

## Ideal customer profile v0 — falsifiable
QA lead, training lead, or operations manager in a customer-support/BPO organization of approximately 30–300 agents; changes operational policies regularly; investigates inconsistent decisions and corrections. Buyer could be QA director or head of operations. Interview to establish frequency, spend, buying process and access.

## First 15–20 conversations
Recruit across at least five independent organizations if reachable, not just sympathetic former colleagues. Source prospects via professional network, LinkedIn and operational communities. Avoid representing employer affiliation or using protected documents. Store raw notes privately.

Ask about a **recent actual case**:
- When did two staff members or systems last interpret a policy differently? What exactly happened?
- Who noticed, and how long did detection take?
- How many cases, complaints, corrections or QA hours followed? Which values are measured versus guesses?
- Who writes, tests, approves and releases the next policy version?
- How often does this happen? What software, spreadsheets or manual QA check currently works?
- Who owns a budget to address it? What competing priorities exist?
- Could you review a fictional version together? Could you legally authorize a sanitized pilot?
Do not open with “Would you buy my AI product?”

## Existing alternatives
Compare real solutions, not imagined gaps: manual review and spreadsheets; ChatGPT/other general AI; SOP/training apps (Scribe, Trainual, Process Street); specialized policy-reasoning technology (e.g. AWS Bedrock Automated Reasoning); contact-center QA products; in-house scripting; consultants. For each, record actual capability, cost, setup, privacy, buyer and test date. Do not say competitors lack a feature without checking.

## Validation criteria by Dec 31, 2026 (planning gate)
- 15 or more substantive interviews attempted, with counts and selection limits documented.
- Multiple recent operational incidents confirmed independently, not hypothetical enthusiasm.
- Two qualified reviewers willing to participate in an authorized, sanitized pilot.
- A plausible identifiable purchaser and purchase process.
If not achieved, narrow/change segment or pause; do not declare success based on compliments.

## Market sizing only after interviews
Estimate reachable organizations bottom-up times price actually tested. Distinguish total market from serviceable reachable market; avoid ungrounded billion-dollar TAM claims. Revisit market and competing substitutes quarterly.

## Public data hygiene
Only anonymized aggregate patterns in this repository. Do not post company names, contact details, recordings, raw quotes, employee names, original SOPs, or customer financial/medical data. Track interview IDs privately with consent and retention rules.
