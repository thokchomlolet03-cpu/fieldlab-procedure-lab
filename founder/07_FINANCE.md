# 07 — Finance, operations, pricing and company readiness
## Status
No established sales or customer economics. Keep actual personal/household figures and customer transaction details OUTSIDE this public repo.

## Private financial ledger schema
date | category | vendor/customer alias | amount | currency | tax | paid/received? | receipt | experiment/contract link | notes.
Categories: domain, hosting, inference, software, hardware, travel, services, legal/accounting, payment fees, insurance, refunds, revenue. Record founder hours separately as opportunity cost; no double-counting.

## Core formulas
Gross margin = (recognized revenue - direct delivery costs) / recognized revenue (only when revenue > 0).
Contribution from one pilot = collected cash - variable expenses to deliver that pilot.
CAC = attributable customer acquisition cost / new customers acquired (too noisy with one customer; label as such).
Runway = unrestricted business cash / monthly net burn, if positive burn; do not assume household money automatically available.
MRR = qualifying recurring monthly revenue, not a one-off invoice or promised trial.

## Spending rules
Every paid service/API must be linked to a named hypothesis, maximum budget, planned result and actual charge. Default to local/synthetic and free tools before confirmed customer need. Never run open-ended model calls or cloud resources without cost controls.

## Company milestones
Pre-sale: discovery, cost log, copyright/IP audit, review employment conditions.
Before accepting payment: determine lawful side-business setup in current jurisdiction, company registration/license if required, taxation, payment rails, invoice/record-keeping and refund terms with advisors.
Before client-data pilot: permission, confidentiality, data terms, deletion and risk allocation.
At repeated sales: bookkeeping reconciliation, gross margin, support hours and customer concentration.
Before investment: cap table/equity review, ownership assignment, corporate structure, diligence-friendly records and qualified counsel.

## Corporate contracts and liability
A customer may outsource a task, but regulatory/legal obligations may remain with them. Do not present FieldLab as transferring liability or providing a certified compliance warranty. Use written bounded pilot scope and reviewed contract protections; coverage depends on jurisdiction, terms and actual controls.

## Full-time checkpoint (2028 only as planning horizon)
Review recurring demand, retention, validated economics, cash runway, family agreement, employment obligations and immigration authorization. A calendar date or anticipated partner income alone is not justification to quit a stable job.
