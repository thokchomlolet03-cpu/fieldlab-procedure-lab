# 09 — Founder cadence, education, YC and longer-term relocation
## Weekly founder rhythm (6–8 hours, adjustable)
- 2 hours customer interviews/outreach/use observations.
- 3 hours highest-value product work.
- 1 hour experiment and financial evidence hygiene.
- 1–2 hours focused learning directly applied to FieldLab.
If shifts, fatigue or other responsibilities reduce available time, lower scope rather than fake progress.

## Friday founder review (or any consistent day)
What did customers actually do? How many independently confirmed real incidents? What assumption was disproved? What was shipped and tested? Cash spent and earned? What security or legal risk is unresolved? What is the ONE experiment next week? What will not be built?

## Monthly board-of-one review
Score: evidence of problem; repeat use; product reliability; sales/pipeline; costs; security/IP; founder sustainability. Record GO / NARROW / PIVOT / PAUSE / STOP with reasons and revisitable criteria. Do not rationalize sunk time or repository size.

## Structured skill curriculum
2026 Q4: talk to users, markets, alternatives, experiments, clear writing.
2027 Q1: scoped MVP, Python/databases/UI/testing, QA and secure deployment basics.
2027 Q2: founder sales, pricing, customer success and product metrics.
2027 H2: distribution, recurring revenue, basic accounting, retention, company/contract operations, hiring/cofounder considerations and pitch preparation.
2028: funding/full-time decision only if supported by evidence.
Learn one topic, APPLY it to a task, record result. No course-completion theater.

## Official founder learning
[YC Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/about) is free education and weekly updates, not investment or immigration status.
[YC library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library), [Do things that don't scale](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/96-do-things-that-don-t-scale), [YC essential startup advice](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/ycs-essential-startup-advice/).
Reading: The Mom Test; The Scout Mindset; The Lean Startup. These are learning suggestions, not required purchases.

## YC readiness package
One-line description, founder-market fit, short honest working demo, exact observed customer problem, why alternatives underperform, product limitations, verified usage/revenue, growth channel, fair technical ownership, team and commitment story, what changed after feedback. Submit at appropriate stage; don't invent traction or hide failed 0/8 experiment.
[Accelerator application](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply) is separate from Startup School. [FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq) says accepted founders are expected to work full time, program in San Francisco. No need to move or incorporate merely to apply.

## Manhattan / USA
Aspirational long-term goal, NOT validation criterion. Startup School alone doesn't provide a U.S. visa or permission to work. A temporary U.S. business visit needs valid entry authorization and does not confer employment rights. YC's accelerator can assist accepted founders with immigration questions but doesn't guarantee status. Possible later routes have distinct criteria (O-1A, IER, qualifying L-1, EB-2 NIW); seek immigration counsel and check then-current official rules. A company must succeed for customers independently of relocation ambitions.
