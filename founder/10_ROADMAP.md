# 10 — Stage-gated founder roadmap (November 2026–2028)
The dates and numerical gates below are **planning experiments, not evidence or commitments**. Re-evaluate quarterly. A pivot, pause or stop can be the best founder decision.

## Phase 0: Discovery — Nov–Dec 2026
Output: reproduce demo; 15–20 interviews across independent companies; dated competitor/substitute matrix; 2 managers agreeing to review a sanitized test; honest incident-level problem statement; private prospect pipeline.
Gate by 2026-12-31: repeated meaningful incidents, identifiable buyer and test participants. If weak, change ICP/problem or pause instead of starting autonomous-agent development.

## Phase 1: Human-reviewed MVP — Jan–Feb 2027
Output: four screens from 03_MVP; immutable policy versions; approved rule/case flow; deterministic edge tests; diff/export, no silent pass; automated and manual testing; external usability observations.
Gate: independent manager completes a real task unassisted and can explain results; engineering results reproduce. No private customer data hosted at this stage.

## Phase 2: Utility pilots — Mar–Jun 2027
Output: 3 bounded authorized pilots (target); fair comparison with spreadsheet and strong general AI on novel cases; time, false alarms, missed issues, reviewed usefulness; attempt first paid offer only if locally permitted.
Gate: objectively useful result or meaningful time advantage, at least one credible buyer path. Otherwise narrow/pivot.

## Phase 3: Repeatability — Jul–Dec 2027
Output: test repeat use on a second policy revision; paid pilot conversion targets; honest unit economics; product support costs and privacy controls; bounded prospecting process.
Gate: evidence of repeated buying/use without growing custom services in lockstep. If not, reassess SaaS thesis.

## Phase 4: Full-time/funding decision — 2028
Output: business case based on actual payments, retention, margin, growth and founder capacity; legal/accounting/IP and family runway review; evaluate YC, cofounder and appropriate U.S. immigration options. Do not quit just because 2028 arrives.

## Next 30 days (Nov 6–Dec 6, 2026)
Week 1: establish project board with issues, reproduce demo, recruit 10 prospects and complete first 3 conversations.
Week 2: 4 more conversations, map 2 real problem patterns into fully fictional test cases.
Week 3: 4 more interviews, conduct guided manual review of one authorized example, refine product scope.
Week 4: 4+ interviews, compare alternatives, write one-page discovery evidence review and revise gate forecast.
Work on software only when it supports the next learning milestone.

## Explicit pivot types
Change customer, problem, delivery model, technical approach or stop. Record the prior claim, evidence against it, successor claim, cost and next falsifiable test. Preserve original history.

## Scoring principle
Closing tasks, producing repositories, reaching 2028, being in YC course, and getting social likes do not prove a business exists. Repeated customer value and credible payments do.
