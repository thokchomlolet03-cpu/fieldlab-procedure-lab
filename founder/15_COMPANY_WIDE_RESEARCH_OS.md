# 15 — FieldLab company-wide research operating system
**Created:** November 6, 2026. **State:** RESEARCH PLAN; not evidence that all studies have been performed. **Scope:** company from discovery to product, operations, scaling and possible financing. **Founder:** solo / part-time, using Google Cloud for development and GitHub for planning.

## Why this exists
A product repository is not a company. This system identifies the work a founder must learn, research, validate, decide, delegate or explicitly defer across every important corporate function. A function appearing here is **not** an instruction to build a department, hire staff, incorporate or buy tools. Solve the most immediate existential uncertainty first.

### Evidence state vocabulary
- **NOT STARTED:** no research has been done.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** plausible belief from founder context; not established.
- **SECONDARY:** documented external sources and competing viewpoints.
- **PRIMARY:** target buyer/user observed or interviewed, with consent and source records.
- **EXPERIMENTAL:** reproducible test, controlled comparison, limitations and negative results.
- **COMMERCIAL:** authorized orders, collected money, repeated use and cost/retention records.
- **OPERATING:** actively maintained policy, contract, system or business process, verified in practice.
A resource list is **not** primary/commercial evidence. Public GitHub contains sanitized conclusions, NOT individual customer contacts or proprietary procedures.

## Essential questions across functions (no silent gaps)
**Who:** ICP, operational user, economic buyer, approver, security/procurement, referrer, operator, eventual founder team.  
**What:** customer job, harm, cost, alternative, product contract, data classification, legal duties and measurable result.  
**Why:** frequency, urgency, willingness to pay, strategic differentiation and reason to act now.  
**How:** build/buy/manual, cloud vs edge, acquisition, deployment, onboarding, support, billing, audit, reliability, exit.  
**How much:** opportunity cost, selling/delivery cost, margin, customer savings, cash runway, taxes, capital/dilution.  
**What could fail:** invalid AI, hallucinated facts, data leakage, buying friction, underpricing, supplier lock-in, new competitor, founder exhaustion, inappropriate work permission.  
**What evidence would reverse us:** pre-registered test criteria, customer purchase refusal, spreadsheet/AI superiority, unrecoverable security risk, unreliable runtime or nonrepeatable labor.

## How to execute research without fake completeness
Each research question becomes an individual record:
`id | function | decision at stake | hypothesis | source type | source/date | evidence summary | limitations | counterevidence | validation action | owner | due/review date | current stage | decision`.
Use [research record template](../founder/templates/COMPANY_RESEARCH_RECORD.md) and keep confidential evidence in an approved private system.

### Research evidence quality
1. Prefer actual customer actions, independent tests and contract/payment receipts to founder confidence.
2. Distinguish official requirements, independent analyses, vendor advertising, anecdotal forum posts and AI-generated speculation.
3. For competitor features, verify a current official source or a dated firsthand demo, and recheck quarterly.
4. Date material facts: current product availability, laws, prices, enterprise requirements and model licenses change.
5. Document contrary data. Record “unknown” rather than infer a company requires a feature.
6. Use actual sampled segment and geographic limits; don't extrapolate 20 interviews into a global market size without uncertainty.
7. Do not expose personal or corporate secrets publicly. Private evidence indexes can point to approved private records without reproducing them.

## Cadence and prioritization
**Weekly NOW:** founder customer conversations, original prototype reliability, competitor/substitute review, single experiment, privacy/IP gate, cash cost.  
**Monthly:** decision review across all active functions; cross-check biggest unknowns and resources.  
**Quarterly:** full function inventory; activate a new function only if a concrete customer/operating event makes it relevant.  
**Annually:** revise strategic thesis, market changes, security/compliance posture, funding/full-time decision.

### Work-in-progress constraint
Only **one primary customer experiment**, **two engineering tasks** and **one founder-learning topic** at any time. A new function's research should answer a decision, not create bureaucracy. No full HR system, investor data room, enterprise certification or extensive brand campaign prior to customer need.

## Stage gates
G0 Discover: real cost/recurrence of problem, reach buyer, compare existing manual/AI alternatives.  
G1 Prototype: independently usable, human-approved rules, truthful deterministic results, no customer confidential data.  
G2 Pilot: data permissions and protections, useful outcomes against baseline, support/contract limitations.  
G3 Sell: lawful setup, paid customer, repeat use, true delivery margin.  
G4 Grow: reliable distribution, customer retention, support, company controls, risk maturity.  
G5 Fund/relocate: optional; funding economics and legal ability, not vanity.

## Explicitly NOT researched or validated yet
A complete customer/market survey, current bottom-up TAM, independent competitor tests, enterprise procurement interviews, model benchmarking on GCP, pricing conversion, recurring revenue, legal entity selection and eventual U.S. work authorization. These are research assignments, not facts established by creating this document.
