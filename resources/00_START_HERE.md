# FieldLab Founder Library — start here
**Updated:** 2026-11-06 | **Status:** curated evolving syllabus, not an exhaustive encyclopedia.
This is a *learning-by-building* library. Follow the stage you are in, avoid trying to finish every resource. Online versions, prices, licenses, and admission requirements change: re-check source before spending or relying on them. Links favor original publishers, official documentation, source repos and public agencies.

## Golden rule for every resource
**READ/WATCH → explain what problem it solves → apply ONE thing to FieldLab → show a resulting artifact → challenge what you learned.** A course completion is not evidence of a viable business.

## Recommended learning method
On finishing a relevant resource, answer in 5 sentences:
- What is the actual underlying problem?
- How did people try to solve it before?
- What approach is advocated, and what assumptions does it rely upon?
- What example from FieldLab could disprove it?
- What concrete change, test or customer conversation will I undertake?
Store personal notes privately; post sanitized links and learning decisions to GitHub issues.

## Access and quality notes
Some books and courses are paid; free previews do not imply free full access. Some vendor tutorials market their products. Government and university references may not cover the founder's jurisdiction. Distinguish opinion/marketing, engineering documentation, measured benchmark and independent evidence.
