# Problem, customer, market and product research
**Highest-priority study for the current stage.** A correct model with no buyer is a research artifact, not a startup.

## Core founder discovery
- **[YC Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/)** — free; do lesson on talking to users, then interview three people that week.
- **[Gustaf Alströmer / YC talks](https://www.ycombinator.com/people/gustaf-alstromer)** — includes "How to Talk to Users" and "How to Get Your First Customers".
- **[Paul Graham: Do Things That Don't Scale](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html)** — begin with a manual service; discover repeated value before automation.
- [The Mom Test](https://www.momtestbook.com/) — paid book, learn to ask about past behavior rather than hypothetical praise.
- [Talking to Humans](https://www.talkingtohumans.com/) — customer discovery book/resource; verify edition/access.
- [Paul Graham: Startup = Growth](https://paulgraham.com/growth.html) — distinguish a startup from consultancy and lifestyle business.
- [First Round Review](https://review.firstround.com/) — founder/customer research and product articles; evaluate editorial claims critically.

## Understand work processes and policies
- **[Amazon SOP-Bench](https://www.amazon.science/blog/sop-bench-a-new-benchmark-for-evaluating-ai-agents-on-real-business-procedures/)** — SOP execution in real-world domains; it does NOT measure customer willingness to pay for FieldLab.
- [SOP-Bench repository](https://github.com/amazon-science/SOP-Bench) — examine actual task ground truth and expert review.
- [AWS Bedrock Automated Reasoning](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/automated-reasoning-checks.html) — significant competitive alternative, verify changing capabilities.
- [Scribe](https://scribehow.com/), [Trainual](https://trainual.com/), [Process Street](https://www.process.st/) — request demos of actual competing workflows; vendor claims are not independent comparisons.
- [Nielsen Norman Group articles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/) — research and usability practice; access varies.
- [Google Ventures Design Sprint](https://www.gv.com/sprint/) — rapid prototype/testing approach; adapt to tiny team.

## Research methods
- [Better Evaluation](https://www.betterevaluation.org/) — practical evaluation design and methods.
- [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/) — evidence literacy and confounders (not market size without source).
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) — research discovery (not verification of quality).
- [Papers with Code](https://paperswithcode.com/) — compare benchmarks and reproducible code; verify datasets and splits.
- [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) — structured risk/validity thinking.

## Stage exercises
1. Write an interview script focused on last actual policy mistake; conduct 15–20 conversations.
2. Record the name of the buyer role, current substitute, frequency, consequences and budget process.
3. In a separate table, compare 5 actual alternative products/manual processes on a fictional common task.
4. Construct a bottom-up market estimate from reachable buyer counts and observed willingness to pay; report uncertainty.
5. Design an independently authored example corpus and a blinded scoring rubric; don't declare product superiority from polished demos.

## Outputs to GitHub
Anonymized incident-pattern report, decision log, competitive dated matrix, product thesis revision and acceptance criteria. Private records and named prospects stay outside public GitHub.
