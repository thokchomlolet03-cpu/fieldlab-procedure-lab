# Marketing, positioning, distribution and founder sales
Study **after/alongside** customer discovery, not instead of it. Buy no course or advertising before verifying that the buyer exists.

## Positioning, communication and buyer understanding
- [Obviously Awesome — April Dunford](https://www.aprildunford.com/obviously-awesome) — paid book; positioning against actual alternatives.
- [April Dunford's official writing](https://www.aprildunford.com/) — exercises on competitors, differentiated value and ideal customer.
- [Made to Stick](https://heathbrothers.com/books/made-to-stick/) — paid book; help people understand a concrete problem without grand AI claims.
- [StoryBrand](https://storybrand.com/) — marketing framework; commercial resource, use critically.
- [YC Library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library) — concise founder messaging and demos.

## Founding sales and B2B distribution
- **[YC Gustaf Alströmer talks](https://www.ycombinator.com/people/gustaf-alstromer)** — first customers and founder-led sales.
- [Stripe Atlas guides — Your First 10 Customers](https://stripe.com/guides/atlas-guides) — practical B2B path and pricing.
- [HubSpot Academy](https://academy.hubspot.com/) — free training options for sales and marketing (some tracks/services may have changing conditions).
- [Close sales blog](https://www.close.com/blog) — tactical outbound sales, vendor perspective.
- [First Round Review](https://review.firstround.com/) — customer success, sales messaging, early GTM.
- [SPIN Selling](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/spin-selling-rackham/M9780070511132.html) — paid book; ask problem/impact questions instead of pitching early.
- [Founding Sales](https://www.foundingsales.com/) — startup sales book/resource; verify latest access terms.

## FieldLab exercises
1. Interview five buyers and write their problem in their own words (keep identifying quotes PRIVATE).
2. Describe FieldLab in one sentence without “agentic”, “revolutionary”, “guaranteed” or “compliant”.
3. Create two landing page variants only after identifying real buyer language; measure useful conversations, not likes.
4. Test a short fixed-price pilot offer with named budget owners; record reason for no.
5. Follow up after next real policy change; observe repeat use rather than self-reported intent.

## Founder sales dashboard (private)
Prospects → meaningful conversations → confirmed incident → actual buyer identified → authorized demo → pilot → invoice → collected payment → repeat use. Distinguish pilot users, sales and nonbuyers. Record founder hours and acquisition cost.

## Avoid
Advertising before repeat usefulness, scraping emails aggressively, fabricated testimonials, claims of ROI without receipts, opening enterprise procurement before controls are ready, and content that reaches other founders but not QA buyers.
