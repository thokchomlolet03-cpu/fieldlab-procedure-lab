## Minimum private finance tracker
Date | category | cash paid/received | currency | tax treatment to confirm | receipt | experiment | related invoice | monthly recurring status | founder labor (separate).
Distinguish gross sales, revenue recognized, cash collection, one-off services vs subscription and profit vs cash.

## Core numerical exercises (use ACTUAL future figures)
- Gross margin = (revenue minus attributable delivery cost)/revenue.
- Pricing floor = direct delivery costs + support cost + expected margin (not necessarily market-acceptable price).
- Cash burn = outflows - inflows; runway only if meaningful business cash and ongoing burn exist.
- Customer acquisition cost = attributable sales+marketing spend divided by newly acquired customers; meaningless as a precise ratio with tiny samples.
- Pilot unit economics: what did it take in cash and founder time to deliver a verified useful finding?
- Model inference cost: true cost per **successful approved review**, including time, installation, device and support, not just token billing.

## Finance decision sequence
Discovery → tally costs without company theater → paid trial only after local legal authorization → invoices and bank reconciliation → repeat customers and customer-level margin → consider incorporation structure, employees, capital requirements → equity/fundraising decisions if growth demands them.

## Governance
Make a monthly founder finance review with receipt-based numbers. Keep any personal income/household budgets, customer details and actual transactions PRIVATE; public repo may contain only template formulas and sanitized aggregate milestones.
