# Legal, company formation, contracts, fundraising, and U.S. founder routes
**Risk note:** This is a source directory, not individualized immigration, employment, securities, privacy or tax advice. Assess applicable UAE, customer and possible future U.S. laws with qualified professionals. Check official requirements before taking action; authorities and forms change.

## Operating legally in the UAE / current jurisdiction
- [UAE official government services](https://u.ae/) — research business licensing, employment, visas and applicable data protection; different free zones have different rules.
- [UAE Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/) — check employment permits, outside work and labor rules. Employment contract/IP assignment may be more restrictive.
- [UAE Federal Tax Authority](https://tax.gov.ae/) — tax registration, corporate tax and VAT as applicable; do not infer thresholds from general articles.
- [UAE Ministry of Economy](https://www.moec.gov.ae/) — corporate/commercial rules and IP guidance; verify jurisdiction.
- [WIPO IP portal](https://www.wipo.int/) — trademarks, patents and copyright concepts; international rights vary.
**Gate:** before payments or real client data, determine lawful business/permit status, IP ownership and invoicing obligations in the founder's actual jurisdiction.

## Privacy, software licensing and contracts
- [NIST CSF 2.0 small-business guide](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1300/final) — baseline security.
- [OWASP ASVS](https://asvs.owasp.org/) — web security controls and verification.
- [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) — AI-specific risk governance.
- [FTC business privacy/security](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security) — U.S. privacy/security fundamentals, not applicable laws everywhere.
- [GDPR official law text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) — only where relevant; formal document, seek interpretation counsel.
- [Choose a License](https://choosealicense.com/) — simplified open-source license comparison, not a substitute for license review.
- [SPDX license list](https://spdx.org/licenses/) — exact license identifiers; audit model weights and software separately.
- [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) — Gemma 4 model card reports Apache 2.0; verify each distribution/dependency.
- [Stripe Atlas startup guides](https://stripe.com/guides/atlas-guides) — U.S.-centric corporation/equity contracts and bookkeeping.
**Gate:** data flow diagram, confidential-data permission, pilot agreement, retention/deletion and third-party AI processor terms BEFORE any actual proprietary SOP is accepted.

## YC and financing
- [YC Startup School FAQ](https://www.startupschool.org/faq) — free online education, not U.S. visa or funding.
- [YC accelerator application](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply) and [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq) — separate competitive investor program.
- [YC Library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library) — fundraising, term sheets, runway, fundraising strategy.
- [Paul Graham essays](https://paulgraham.com/articles.html) — early founder/investor perspective.
- [Stripe Atlas guides](https://stripe.com/guides/atlas-guides) — pitching, founder equity and financing (jurisdiction caveats).
- [Carta Learn](https://carta.com/learn/) — cap tables, equity and dilution; vendor perspective, verify terms.
- [SEC small business capital raising](https://www.sec.gov/smallbusiness) — U.S. securities and exemptions; financing requires qualified counsel.
- [NVCA resources](https://nvca.org/resources/) — venture capital model documents and policy resources; professional review mandatory.
- [SAFE financing documents (YC)](https://www.ycombinator.com/documents) — current documentation and local variations; understand post-money dilution.
**Gate:** first validate customer demand; fundraising is a tool for a justified growth plan, not proof of value. Track fully diluted ownership and costs before signing any term sheet.

## Visiting or moving to the United States
- [US Department of State visa site](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas.html) — visitor categories and consular process.
- [DS-160 information](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/forms/ds-160-online-nonimmigrant-visa-application.html) — apply only for truthful allowed purpose.
- [USCIS Entrepreneurs](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/entrepreneurs) — founder options and limits, eligibility changes.
- [USCIS O-1](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/o-1-visa-individuals-with-extraordinary-ability-or-achievement) — high evidentiary threshold.
- [USCIS International Entrepreneur Rule](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/international-entrepreneur-rule) — discretionary qualifying pathway, not automatically a green card.
- [USCIS EB-2 / NIW](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/permanent-workers/employment-based-immigration-second-preference-eb-2) — eligibility and national interest considerations.
- [USCIS L-1](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/l-1a-intracompany-transferee-executive-or-manager) — qualifying foreign/U.S. employer relationship; not granted for a paper company.
**Gate:** NYC is a personal aspiration and must not shape inflated product claims. Startup School ≠ U.S. residency. YC accelerator operates in San Francisco. Immigration counsel is needed when the business truly supports a route.

## Startup risk and vendor procurement
Study agreements, data processing, confidentiality, scope limitations, warranties, insurance, liability caps, indemnities, procurement and service obligations. Buyers may outsource work yet retain regulatory responsibility. No customer contract or legal conclusion should be copied blindly from a generic template.

## Deliverables
A private compliance/ownership checklist; versioned terms and privacy docs when a legal entity/pilot requires them; an investor readiness memo based on real metrics; an immigration options memo drafted with current counsel when appropriate.
