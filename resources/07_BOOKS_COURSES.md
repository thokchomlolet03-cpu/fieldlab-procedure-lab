# Books, courses, essays and leadership — by need, not completion badges
Status: curated suggestions, not mandatory purchases. **FREE / PAID / MIXED** are approximate and should be verified before enrollment.

## Founder essentials — read NOW
1. **[YC Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/)** — FREE; talk to users, evaluate startup ideas, MVP and growth. Apply one lesson weekly.
2. **[The Mom Test — Rob Fitzpatrick](https://www.momtestbook.com/)** — PAID book; practical non-leading interviews. Output: stronger interview script.
3. **[Do Things That Don't Scale — Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html)** — FREE essay; try manual policy testing before over-automating.
4. **[Startup = Growth — Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com/growth.html)** — FREE essay; understand scalable growth versus consultancy.
5. **The Scout Mindset — Julia Galef** — PAID book; model updating and defensiveness. Output: one documented belief changed by criticism.
6. **The Lean Startup — Eric Ries** — PAID book; experiments and pivot logic. Output: pre-committed criteria for one risky claim.
7. **[Talking to Humans](https://www.talkingtohumans.com/)** — access may vary; customer conversations and problem definition.

## Building software and product
- **[MIT Missing Semester](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/)** — FREE, hands-on developer tooling, Git and debugging.
- **[CS50](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/)** — FREE to learn, certificate terms vary; core CS skills.
- **[MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/)** — FREE, theoretical and engineering topics; select only relevant coursework.
- **[FastAPI tutorial](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/tutorial/)** — FREE, the core of FieldLab web app.
- [Don't Make Me Think — Steve Krug](https://sensible.com/dont-make-me-think/) — PAID, basic usability.
- [The Design of Everyday Things — Don Norman](https://jnd.org/the-design-of-everyday-things-revised-and-expanded-edition/) — PAID, human-centered design and feedback.
- [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://dataintensive.net/) — PAID, later infrastructure systems engineering.
- [OSTEP](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/) — FREE OS book; understand local process/storage and failure.

## How to use a book
For every chapter, produce a concrete FieldLab action: rewrite an interview question, choose a smaller feature, change a pricing offer, test a UI, recalculate direct delivery cost. Do not collect completed books as a status trophy.
