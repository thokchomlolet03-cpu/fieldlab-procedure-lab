# Company-wide research and reference library — all major functions
**Created:** Nov 6, 2026 | **Research status:** curated leads, not completed findings.
Read [function inventory, stage gates and evidence states](../founder/15_COMPANY_WIDE_RESEARCH_OS.md). The earlier [Founder Learning Index](00_START_HERE.md) covers technical/business study. This page expands corporate functions and *research outputs*. Prices, law and product capabilities must be checked at the date of each actual decision.

## Strategy, business model, competitive advantage
**Learn:** [Business Model Canvas / Strategyzer](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas) (tool/vendor perspective); [HBR article explaining the canvas](https://hbr.org/2013/05/a-better-way-to-think-about-yo) (business model structure); [Paul Graham: Startup = Growth](https://paulgraham.com/growth.html); [YC library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library); [SBA plan your business](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business).
**Research:** value chain; substitutes; pricing powers; expansion vs focus; partnership leverage; build vs buy. **Produce:** one-page thesis, rival theses, dated competitive map, bottom-up reachable market.

## Customer, product and experience
**Learn:** [YC Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/); [The Mom Test](https://www.momtestbook.com/) (paid book); [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/); [WCAG accessibility standard](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/); [SOP-Bench](https://www.amazon.science/blog/sop-bench-a-new-benchmark-for-evaluating-ai-agents-on-real-business-procedures/).
**Research:** decision-maker mapping, user job, workflow errors, accessibility, friction and trust in AI recommendations. **Produce:** anonymized interviews, journey, four-screen prototype, unassisted task test.

## Engineering, reliability, data, model R&D
**Learn:** [Google Cloud architecture guidance](https://cloud.google.com/architecture/framework), [FastAPI](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/), [pytest](https://docs.pytest.org/), [Gemma documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs), [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), [OWASP ASVS](https://asvs.owasp.org/), [Google SRE book](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/).
**Research:** source-grounding, eval outcomes, deterministic rule engine, offline/network leakage, GCP region and spend, failure/rollback. **Produce:** frozen benchmark, CI, private service, threat-model, measured cost per approved review. GCP is the dev host; GCP inference is NOT customer-edge privacy.

## Sales, marketing, positioning and growth
**Learn:** [YC founder advice](https://www.ycombinator.com/library), [HubSpot Academy](https://academy.hubspot.com/), [April Dunford positioning](https://www.aprildunford.com/), [Google SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide), [SBA marketing and sales](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business), [Founding Sales](https://www.foundingsales.com/).
**Research:** buyer sourcing, sales cycle, partner referrals, buying friction, CAC, conversion, target messages. **Produce:** buyer funnel, objections, one bounded paid offer and actual close/loss reasons.

## Customer success, account management, support
**Learn:** [SBA manage your business](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business), [Intercom customer support resources](https://www.intercom.com/blog/) (vendor perspective), [Google SRE incident principles](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/), [GOV.UK service manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual) (government service operations).
**Research:** onboarding, repeat policy revision usage, trained vs untrained outcome, support response ownership, renewals, complaints, escalation and incident communication. **Produce:** pilot onboarding checklist, post-pilot report, retention cohort and runbook. No artificial “customer success department” before customers.

## Finance, accounting, tax and financial strategy
**Learn:** [Khan Academy finance](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/core-finance), [OpenStax business textbooks](https://openstax.org/subjects/business), [SBA costs and managing](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide), [Stripe Atlas](https://stripe.com/guides/atlas-guides), [UAE Federal Tax Authority](https://tax.gov.ae/).
**Research:** pricing unit, labor delivery costs, subscriptions vs one-time revenue, cloud/API costs, taxes and business setup, runway, margin, working capital, vendor lock-in. **Produce:** PRIVATE monthly ledger, actual cost-per-successful-review and pricing experiment. Do not store household finances in public GitHub.

## Legal, corporate governance, board duties, IP
**Learn:** [UAE official portal](https://u.ae/), [UAE MOHRE](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/), [UAE FTA](https://tax.gov.ae/), [WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/), [SPDX licenses](https://spdx.org/licenses/), [SBA launch](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business), [YC SAFE](https://www.ycombinator.com/documents).
**Research:** outside work/employer IP, licensing, trade names, company formation, founder equity, board authority, customer warranties, product risk, data agreements and applicable tax. **Produce:** jurisdiction-specific legal review and action-triggered controls. References here are not legal advice and U.S. guidance may not apply to the UAE.

## Hiring, HR, compensation, organizational design
**Learn:** [U.S. SBA hire and manage employees](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business/hire-manage-employees) (U.S. example), [SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/) (mix of membership/paid materials), [Google re:Work](https://rework.withgoogle.com/) (people research), [UAE MOHRE](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/), [YC founder/cofounder advice](https://www.ycombinator.com/library).
**Research:** build/hire/outsource choices, skill gaps, cofounder selection, fair hiring, compensation, contractor vs employee classification, onboarding, offboarding and access removal. **Produce:** role/skills matrix and employment advice only when staffing needs are real. Never imply a team has been hired.

## Internal operations, process quality and leadership
**Learn:** [SBA managing operations](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business), [Google SRE](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/), [MIT OpenCourseWare management](https://ocw.mit.edu/search/?q=management), [HBR strategy implementation](https://hbr.org/2006/03/how-to-implement-a-new-strategy-without-disrupting-your-organization).
**Research:** decision cadence, role accountability, incident response, change management, documented work, handoffs, founder bottlenecks. **Produce:** weekly review, monthly board-of-one decisions, SOPs only where risk demands them.

## Risk, security, privacy and corporate compliance
**Learn:** [NIST CSF](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework), [NIST ICT supplier due diligence 2026](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1326/final), [NIST C-SCRM](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/cyber-supply-chain-risk-management), [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), [OWASP LLM risks](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/).
**Research:** vendor trust, model supply chain, asset ownership, policy leakage, data localization, customer liability, security assurance, incident response, business continuity. **Produce:** data flow map, vendor register, privacy policy only when applicable, documented residual risks.

## Enterprise procurement, vendor and partner economics
**Learn:** [NIST supplier due diligence](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1326/final), [NIST C-SCRM quick-start](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1305/final), [GSA acquisition resources](https://www.gsa.gov/buy-through-us) (U.S. public sector example, not early target), [AWS Bedrock Automated Reasoning docs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/automated-reasoning-checks.html) (technology competitor).
**Research:** buying authority, internal security/procurement questionnaires, insurance/indemnity demands, authorized data processor requirements, vendor screening, SLA expectations. **Produce:** buyer procurement journey and minimum viable trust/assurance pack. Do not make speculative enterprise SOC 2 spending a prerequisite to talking to small pilot buyers.

## Funding, investors, ownership, capital structure
**Learn:** [YC Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/), [YC accelerator application](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply), [YC SAFE docs](https://www.ycombinator.com/documents), [SEC small business funding](https://www.sec.gov/smallbusiness), [NVCA resources](https://nvca.org/resources/), [Carta educational resources](https://carta.com/learn/) (vendor).
**Research:** bootstrap vs external financing, accelerator requirements, ownership/dilution, investment instrument obligations, founder vesting, cost to reach meaningful scale. **Produce:** financing alternatives decision memo, cap-table scenario and honest YC application ONLY if business merits.

## International expansion and relocation
**Learn:** [USCIS entrepreneur resources](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/entrepreneurs), [U.S. Department of State visas](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas.html), [UAE official government](https://u.ae/), [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq).
**Research:** real customer geography, region/privacy costs, export and licensing constraints, transfer pricing as applicable, founder legal right to operate in destination country and immigration routes. **Produce:** location strategy with lawyer-reviewed eligibility, not the assumption that YC Startup School confers U.S. work rights.

## Founder ethics, resilience and research integrity
**Learn:** *The Scout Mindset* (Julia Galef); [YC feedback/founder materials](https://www.ycombinator.com/library); [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework); [The Mom Test](https://www.momtestbook.com/).
**Research:** cognitive bias, overbuilding, conflict of interest, honest claims, dual-use policy applications, work sustainability, customer harm. **Produce:** public admission of limitations, pre-registered evaluation, monthly go/narrow/pivot decision and explicit stop rules.

## Source verification rules
Official regulatory sources define regulatory facts; independent buyer observations define customer behavior; software tests define what code did under known conditions; investor/consultant/vendor commentary is a starting hypothesis. Record the publication/check date. Do not infer actual customer contracts, prices, product capability or legal advice from generic educational material.

## Defer list for a solo founder without validated customers
Full HR department, large corporate governance structure, SOC 2 certification purchase, patent filings on speculation, complicated SaaS pricing tiers, international expansion, corporate tax structuring, enterprise integrations, paid ads and fundraising tours. Research what is necessary before taking an irreversible step.
